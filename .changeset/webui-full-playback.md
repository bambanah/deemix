Add full-track playback for logged-in users. Albums and playlists can be played from the tracklist through a player bar that streams and decrypts Deezer tracks on the server, with seeking, a FLAC/MP3 quality setting and a badge showing the format being played. A new Playback page holds the streaming quality and the Last.fm connection, which scrobbles played tracks using either the track artist or the album artist. Also shows the total length of a tracklist and replaces the app logo and favicon.
